Meridian Industrial Ltd - Q2FY26 Results Press Release
17 October 2025

Meridian Industrial Limited (NSE: MERIDIAN, BSE: 532961), a manufacturer of
precision flow-control equipment for the energy, water and process industries,
announced results for the quarter ended 30 September 2025.

Highlights for Q2FY26:
- Revenue of Rs. 3,142 crore, up 16.8% YoY and 5.4% QoQ
- EBITDA of Rs. 561 crore, up 24.1% YoY; EBITDA margin 17.9% (up 110bps YoY)
- Profit after tax of Rs. 372 crore, up 27.4% YoY
- Order inflow of Rs. 4,010 crore; closing order book at a record Rs. 18,600 crore
- Export revenue up 29.2% YoY, now 34.1% of the mix
- Net debt reduced to Rs. 1,665 crore from Rs. 2,140 crore in Q2FY25

Sector: Industrial Engineering
